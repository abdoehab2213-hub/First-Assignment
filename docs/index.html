1. Title & Abstract

Title: Automated Search Traffic Decay Detection & Action Prioritization Engine

Abstract: Maintaining organic search visibility at scale requires continuous identification of underperforming pages. This research presents a machine learning framework designed to predict traffic decay across high-impression web pages using historical search performance metrics. By deploying a Random Forest classifier trained on 90-day search performance data, our model achieves an ROC-AUC of 0.6772, significantly outperforming rule-based heuristics (ROC-AUC 0.5198). The model automates triage decisions into actionable categories: CONTENT_REFRESH, SNIPPET_FIX, and CONTENT_REVIEW.

2. Problem Statement & Decision Context

Organic search decay leads to significant loss of digital traffic and revenue. Manual audit of thousands of URLs is unscalable and inefficient. This project builds an automated decision-support pipeline that evaluates page-level search performance to direct content and SEO teams toward high-ROI optimization tasks.

3. Data & Privacy Framework

  • Dataset Source: FlyRank Search Intelligence Anonymized Dataset (30,000 raw URL records).
  • Window & Scope: 90-day aggregation window for impression and click metrics.
  • Public Safety & Privacy: All URLs and personal domain identifiers are fully anonymized via secure url_hash cryptographic identifiers.
  • Noise Exclusion: Filtered out low-visibility pages (< 500 impressions over 90 days), retaining 16,726 high-visibility pages for robust modeling.

4. Methodology & Leakage Guardrails

We engineered key interaction ratios using strictly historical, pre-decision metrics:

  • ctr_pos_ratio: Measures click efficiency relative to rank position.
  • staleness_years: Normalizes content age from days to annual scale.
  • traffic_density: Captures conversion density per impression.

Leakage Audit: Zero target-derived features or future analytical windows were included in the training feature matrix.

6. Limitations & Honest Analytical Framing

  • Observational Nature: Predictions provide directional prioritization and do not establish direct causal reverse-engineering of search ranking algorithms.
  • Unobserved Variables: Search query seasonality, major search engine core algorithm updates, and external competitor optimizations are outside the dataset scope.
  • Human-in-the-Loop: Action labels serve as prioritization prompts requiring manual editorial review prior to content modifications.

7. Ranked Action Playbook Output

Using the composite Capstone Score ($Probability \times Impressions$), we evaluated 16,726 pages and generated 13,123 prioritized recommendations:

  • CONTENT_REVIEW: 12,536 pages (General high decay risk)
  • SNIPPET_FIX: 571 pages (Page 1 ranking with sub-2% CTR)
  • CONTENT_REFRESH: 16 pages (High decay risk with age ≥ 180 days)
  • MONITOR: 3,603 pages (Stable performance)
